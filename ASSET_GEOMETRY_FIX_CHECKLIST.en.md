## General rules

**Normalize formula for each pack** (only the transforms permitted by spec §12.1 — scale + translate, NO redraw):

1. Scale by **a single uniform factor `s` for all 5 stages** (preserving the within-species progression), LANCZOS resample, keep RGBA.
2. Translate **each stage individually** so the root lands on exactly `(512, 970)` on the `1024×1024` canvas.
3. `s` is computed automatically by `tools/normalize_pack.py` = the minimum of the constraints that keep every pixel ≥ 24 px away from the edge, computed from the actual extent around each stage's root (it does not assume the root sits at the center of the bbox).

**Acceptance criteria** (verify with `python3 tools/geometry_audit.py masters/farm/<class>/<species>/*.png`):

- Canvas matches the target; contactY = 970, Δ = 0 (except crops — soil-plate anchor, spec §5.3).
- rootX = 512 ± 2 px; radial/rosette verified by eye (spec §7.1).
- No pixel closer than 24 px to the edge; no clipping; the style does not break after resampling.
- Verify on the raw PNG, not only through the playground (spec §12.3).
